# FAB Prepaid Card Balance Enquiry Guide

By Daniel Collins

A prepaid card is useful only when you know exactly how much money is left on it. This guide explains how to check a First Abu Dhabi Bank prepaid card balance in the most practical ways, how to read the result, and what to do when the figure looks out of date.

## Checking a prepaid card balance online

The online route is usually the clearest option because it shows the card balance together with recent activity. Start by opening the official FAB website or the card portal linked from it, then sign in with the credentials you set up when the card was activated. Choose the prepaid card from your list of cards, and open its details page. The available balance should appear near the top, with separate lines for recent purchases, refunds and fees where relevant.

For a clear walkthrough, use this [fab card enquiry](https://uaecheckbalance.com/fab-prepaid-cards-enquiry/) guide alongside the steps on the official portal. It helps to check the balance before a large purchase, especially for travel, hotel holds or online orders, because a temporary hold can reduce the amount that is actually available even though the headline balance looks higher.

## Checking by SMS and in the app

If you are away from a computer, SMS and the mobile app are good backups. For SMS, use the mobile number registered with the bank and follow the balance enquiry format shown in the official FAB guidance for your card type. The reply usually arrives within a couple of minutes and shows the available balance at that moment.

In the app, sign in, select the prepaid card, and look for the balance on the card summary screen. The app is often better than SMS for spotting a problem, because you can open the transaction history and see whether a payment is still pending, whether a refund has arrived, or whether a fee was applied after your last check.

## Reading the result correctly

Three figures can cause confusion on prepaid cards. The first is the total balance, the second is the available balance, and the third is any amount held for a pending transaction. Always rely on the available balance when you decide whether you can pay. A hotel, car rental company or online store can place a hold that is larger than the final charge, and that hold will lower the available balance until it is released.

Also check the date and time of the last update. Some portals refresh instantly, while others update after a batch of transactions is processed. If you have just topped up the card, give the top up a short time to appear, then refresh the page or reopen the card in the app.

## What to do if the enquiry shows an old balance

An old balance is common after a recent top up, refund or purchase, but it should not be ignored if it stays wrong. Work through these steps in order:

1. Refresh the page, sign out and sign back in, and check the card again. A stored page can show yesterday's figure.
2. Open the full transaction history and look for the missing top up, refund or purchase. Note the date, amount and merchant name.
3. Check whether the money went to a different card or account, especially if you hold more than one prepaid card.
4. Wait one full working day for a bank transfer or refund to settle, because weekends and public holidays can delay processing.
5. If the balance is still wrong, contact FAB through the number on the back of the card or the secure message option in the app. Give the card's last digits, the date and amount of the transaction, and a screenshot or reference number if you have one.

Do not share your full card number, PIN, OTP or password with anyone who contacts you about the balance. The bank will not ask for those details by phone, email or social media.
